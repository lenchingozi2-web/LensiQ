# Live Class outcomes

- [ ] **Legacy Word uploads work across lecture flows.** Accept and parse real `.doc` files as well as PDF, DOCX, PPTX, and TXT; normalize MIME variants and detect extensionless Word files by binary signature while keeping the 20-file/50 MB limits clear.
- [ ] **The Live Class canvas is premium and full-bleed.** `/voice?mode=class` badges into a dark medical-lab stage with restrained chrome and a centered frosted-glass LiveKit dock.
- [ ] **Visuals follow the active concept, not a fixed course theme.** The tutor and transcript router can move between relevant anatomy, pharmacology targets, chemical-pathology/ADME whiteboards, physiology simulations, pathology or histology/cytology schematics, supplied media, and explanation-specific concept boards; unrelated prior visuals do not linger.
- [ ] **The approved medical catalog is available to the tutor.** Visual metadata includes exact asset URLs, model-specific ligand cautions and attribution, allowed commands, and learner feedback events; unapproved model URLs and unavailable imagery are not presented as available.
- [ ] **Learners can explore the lesson.** They can rotate/zoom models, focus structures, inspect media, and select/zoom whiteboard concepts while the transcript and visual stage stay synchronized.
- [ ] **A useful asset set is seeded with storage headroom preserved.** Ship valid anatomy/system models, pharmacology structures, and clearly labelled educational histology/cytology/pathology schematics with per-asset rights metadata under the conservative project-storage budget.
